# Earth Information Jukebox Ideas

## Deferred experiences

### Blindfold Mode

Hide the visualization for the first loop and ask the listener to identify the Earth phenomenon from sound alone. Reveal the frame, NASA explanation, and listening cues after the guess.

Possible flow:

1. Choose a five-track challenge.
2. Play one loop without showing the image or title.
3. Offer three phenomenon choices.
4. Reveal the visualization and explain which sound mappings were clues.
5. Show a final score without requiring an account.

Estimated prototype: 90 minutes.

### Shareable Earth Mixtapes

Let a listener arrange three jukebox records into a short set and give it a title. Export a package that keeps the NASA science attached to the music.

Possible outputs:

- A WAV mix.
- A generated cover image.
- Liner notes describing each NASA dataset and its sound mapping.
- Source links and required attribution.

Estimated prototype: 4–6 hours.
